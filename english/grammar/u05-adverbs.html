讲解

副词 Adverbs · 动笔前看两分钟

副词说动作怎么样He runs fast. · She speaks politely.形容词说人（a fast runner），副词说动作（runs fast）；why · how 也是副词
短副词加 -er / -estfast → faster → fastest · early → earlier → earliesty 结尾的先把 y 换成 i。fast · hard · late · early 走这条
-ly 的加 more / mostslowly → more slowly → most slowly
三个不按规则的well → better → best · badly → worse → worst · far → farther → farthest
一样 / 不如：as … asMary runs as fast as I do. · I don't skate as well as Jim.两个 as 中间放原级，不变形
比用 than，最用 in / ofShe gets up earlier than I. · Lucy sings best in our class.than 前面放比较级，in / of 前面放最高级
时间从小往大排Let's meet at seven thirty on Sunday morning.先说几点，再说哪天 —— 和中文正好反过来
