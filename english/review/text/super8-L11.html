  2. 2第 59–60 页

    "You see, Matthew is getting old, and with his poor heart, it would be nice to have a helper," Marilla explained.

  3. 3第 60 页

    Mrs. Lynde couldn't hide her shock and dislike. "You are actually inviting troubles in by adopting a totally strange boy!"

  4. 4第 60 页

    At the train station, to Matthew Cuthbert's surprise, it was not a boy as he had expected. The stationmaster pointed to a girl waiting for him.

  5. 5第 60 页

    "Mrs. Spencer must have made a mistake," thought Matthew. "But I can't just tell the girl so."

  6. 6第 60 页

    Yet when they were approaching home, worries started to grow on his mind about how the truth might upset this spirited girl.

  7. 7第 61 页

    "Matthew, what's the problem?" she inquired, eyes wide open. "Where's the boy promised by Mrs. Spencer?"

  8. 8第 61 页

    "It was Anne Shirley," the girl said, emphasizing the "Anne" with an "E". "That way, the name looks more special."

  9. 9第 61 页

    Marilla chuckled at Anne's remarks, but she couldn't let go of the mistake Mrs. Spencer made, so she kept asking whether there were any other orphans brought along with Anne.

  10. 10第 61 页

    "There was only Lily Jones, who Mrs. Spencer brought for herself," Anne responded. She thought Marilla wanted a beautiful girl with nut-brown hair, but it turned out what she really needed was a boy.

  11. 11第 62 页

    Anne knew what was going to happen, but she tried hard to be happy on the way to Mrs. Spencer's. She enjoyed the trip, especially when she saw a bright pink wild rose. She really liked the color pink.

  12. 12第 62 页

    She even quoted the sentence "My life is a graveyard of buried hopes." from a book, announcing that was what comforted her when she lost heart.

  13. 13第 62 页

    Marilla asked, "Anne, would you please introduce who you are?"

  14. 14第 62 页

    "I came from Nova Scotia and I turned 11 in March last year," Anne said.

  15. 15第 63 页

    She continued, saying her parents were both teachers in high schools. The family was very poor and she was born in a tiny yellow house. When she was only three months old, her mother and father died of fever successively.

  16. 16第 63 页

    "As an orphan, I lived with Mrs. Thomas until eight years old, and then her husband died in a train accident, so I had to leave and lived with the Hammonds for two years."

  17. 17第 63 页

    "Then Mr. Hammond died and I was no longer needed, so I was sent to the orphanage."

  18. 18第 63 页

    Mrs. Spencer advised sending Anne to serve Mrs. Blewett, who was known for being bad-tempered and cruel to her servant girls.

  19. 19第 64 页

    Marilla chose to keep her decision to let Anne stay a secret until the next day. Before telling Anne, Marilla thought she needed to test her first.

  20. 20第 64 页

    She first demanded Anne to pick up her clothes from the floor, fold them, and put them on the chair.

  21. 21第 64 页

    Then she asked her to say her prayers before going to bed.

  22. 22第 64 页

    Anne gave it a try by thanking God and asking for things that were important to her: allowing her to stay at Green Gables and growing up into a pretty woman.

  23. 23第 65 页

    When that job was done, Marilla finally admitted that she and Matthew had decided to keep Anne.

  24. 24第 65 页

    Then Anne expressed her wish to find a close friend she could trust and share everything with.

  25. 25第 65 页

    "Diana Barry living nearby could be a good choice," Marilla said. "She is of your age, and is nice and good." Meanwhile, she warned that to be friends with Diana, Anne must behave herself.

  26. 26第 66 页

    Then one day, Mrs. Lynde dropped in on Marilla. She asked too many questions about Anne. Understanding the aim of Mrs. Lynde's visit, Marilla called Anne back.

  27. 27第 66 页

    Mrs. Lynde called Anne to come closer, but Anne was already angry after hearing the remarks about her appearance.

  28. 28第 67 页

    "You could lock me in a dungeon and punish me anyway you want, but I will not say sorry to that woman," she said determinedly.

  29. 29第 67 页

    Anne asked Marilla to take her to Mrs. Lynde's. There, in her unique way of apologizing, Anne kneeled in front of the woman, exclaiming her regret about scolding her and saying all those bad things about her.

  30. 30第 67 页

    "Although those things were true, I shouldn't have said them. Can you please, please forgive me?" Anne was having fun of her own by doing this.

  31. 31第 68 页

    At the Barrys', Anne met Diana, a very pretty girl. The two sat in the garden, appreciating the gorgeous flowers around. Gathering up her courage, Anne voiced her wish. "Diana, can can we be best friends?"

  32. 32第 68 页

    Diana nodded with a smile. "I'd love to have a good friend of my age."

  33. 33第 68 页

    It was said that a Sunday school picnic would be held next Wednesday, with Mrs. Lynde joining in to make ice cream. Anne heard the news and pleaded with Marilla to let her go.

  34. 34第 68 页

    "I will prepare something for you,"

  35. 35第 68–69 页

    On Tuesday, Marilla found the precious brooch gone. Knowing that Anne was also fond of it, she asked if she had seen it.

  36. 36第 69 页

    "Yes, I saw it this afternoon and tried it on," Anne admitted, but stressed that she had put it back.

  37. 37第 69 页

    Wednesday was the day of the picnic. In order to be quickly set free from her room, Anne made a false confession to Marilla, saying she did take the brooch and lost it in a river.

  38. 38第 69 页

    But later, Marilla found the brooch while mending a black scarf. She realized that Anne only confessed to go to the picnic. "Anne, it was wrong for you to lie about losing the brooch, but it was also wrong for me to not believe in you in the very beginning," Marilla managed to patch things up with Anne.

  39. 39第 70 页

    When September came, Anne started her studies at school. Marilla was worried that she may have a hard time adapting to school. However, Anne spent her first day smoothly and came home glowing.

  40. 40第 70 页

    "I received the first praise in my life!" She couldn't wait to share it with Marilla. "Some classmates said my nose is pretty!" But she didn't quite like her teacher Mr. Philipps, who publicly criticized her spelling in class.

  41. 41第 70 页

    "Let's see what you've got," he said to himself as he approached Anne. He grabbed her braid, calling it "carrots" loudly in front of the whole class. This instantly angered Anne. "You sneaky little boy!" She fetched her slate and cracked it on Gilbert's head.

  42. 42第 71 页

    But Mr. Philipps didn't listen to this. He instructed Anne to stand in front of the board for the rest of the afternoon. He even wrote words criticizing Anne's behavior on the board, encouraging the entire class to read them aloud.

  43. 43第 71 页

    Mr. Philipps instructed Anne to sit with Gilbert after he spotted her speaking to another boy. Anne refused to move at first, but eventually obeyed the order. But at that moment, she had already decided to never go back to school, so she took everything out of her desk after school.

  44. 44第 71 页

    "I'm not going back to school, ever again." Anne told Marilla the minute she arrived home. Marilla tried to change her mind, but Anne was not swayed. Marilla saw no point in arguing with Anne, so she agreed for the moment.

  45. 45第 72 页

    On an October morning, Marilla advised that Anne invite Diana over for tea in the afternoon when she went into town.

  46. 46第 72 页

    "I have made some cake and raspberry cordial," Marilla said. "They are in the pantry. Help yourselves."

  47. 47第 72 页

    "The cordial is tasty!" Diana was impressed after taking a sip. "It's so much better than what I had before."

  48. 48第 72 页

  49. 49第 72 页

    Diana said she felt dizzy and excused herself to leave.

  50. 50第 72–73 页

    The next day, Anne heard from Mrs. Lynde that Diana's mother was very displeased with Anne, because Anne made her daughter drunk.

  51. 51第 73 页

    Then it occurred to her that the cordial was actually in the cellar, not in the pantry as she told Anne.

  52. 52第 73 页

    Anne couldn't take the hit and cried herself to sleep back home. Marilla felt a deep compassion for her.

  53. 53第 74 页

    While they were chatting in the kitchen, Diana came in hurriedly, looking worried.

  54. 54第 74 页

    "Diana! What brought you here?" asked Anne. "Is something wrong?"

  55. 55第 74 页

    "It's my little sister Minnie May," Diana said, puffing and crying. "She's got croup, but my parents are both out of town to see the premier, so there's no one to get the doctor!"

  56. 56第 74 页

    Hearing this, Matthew instantly left home for the doctor. Anne was quickly getting ready to leave too. "Don't worry, I know how to handle croup. When I stayed at the Hammonds', the babies I looked after all had it," she comforted Diana.

  57. 57第 74 页

    When they arrived at the Barrys' house on a snowy night, Anne discovered that Minnie May was running a high fever and having difficulty breathing. She instructed Diana to bring plenty of hot water and then helped undress Minnie May and gave her several servings of ipecac.
