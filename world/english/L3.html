🌟 读得好的句子15 句

  • 第二句 一字不差：“Take great care of this ring,” she said. “It is more precious than diamonds.”

    precious / diamonds / disappeared / friendship / continue 这些长词，还有 “Prince Darling the Happy” 这个名字，全都一次读对。

  • 最长的那句 50 个词，从 “The poor little dog, who didn’t understand this at all…”

    一路到 “…he gave her a hard kick.”，**一个词都没读错**。 didn’t / Darling’s / Instantly / sharply / deserve / ill-treated 这些词都是一次读对的。

  • 奶妈那句话 “Remember, my dear, one day you will be King. As a king, you can do whatever you please. No one will have the power to stop you.” —— 27 个词，一个没错。

    foolish / nurse / whatever / unhappy / struggle / against / punished 这些词都是一次读对的。

  • 第二段整段 “He wanted to be able to do as he liked, so he threw his ring aside, … to have got rid of its teasing pricks.” —— 30 个词只错 thought 一处，happiest / teasing / pricks 都是一次读对的。

  • “If I see you commit wrongdoings every day, I will dislike you” —— wrongdoings 这个 11 个字母的长词读得干脆。

  • 跳段读的那半句 “Do you dislike me?” asked —— 虽然读错了地方，那句本身一个字没错，dislike 也对。

  • 回读的第二遍 “and was determined to be a better man in order to please her.” —— 13 个词一字不差，determined 一次到位。

  • “encouraged him in all his evil ways” —— encouraged 读得干脆，没有拆。

  • 这一页最后一句 “to teach the other people that you mean to be obeyed.” —— 11 个词一个没错，obeyed 也对。

  • “He loved the prince as if he had been his own son and wished that he would always take the path of goodness.” —— 96 到 118 秒，27 个词一路读到底，一处没错，是三页里最长的一段干净朗读。

  • “You ungratefully turned upon your tutor, who was like a second father to you.” —— ungratefully 这个五音节的词一次到位，整句 14 个词全对。

  • “The prince, in great anger, ordered his foster-brother to bring Suliman back with a troop of soldiers.” —— 160 到 180 秒，除了名字全对，troop 和 soldiers 都读出来了。

  • “The prince decided to show his sorrow for his past bad behavior by being gentle with the man tasked to care for him.” —— 21 个词一路读到底，behavior、gentle、tasked 都一次到位。

  • “Even though the poor beast did what he was told, the caretaker often hit him for no reason just because he was in a bad mood.” —— 136 秒到 190 秒这 54 秒里一处没错，是整段最稳的一截。

  • 课本把 eat 印成了 cat，她读的是 eat；书上 thunderbolt 那个词又长又生，她也读了出来 —— 印错的、难的，都没把她带跑。
