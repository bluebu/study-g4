🌟 读得好的句子17 句

  • There was another event that I will always remember.

    一处没错，always remember 连着读出来。

  • He said I was the right horse for him

    这句没停没错。

  • One morning, Squire Gordon bought me because he thought I was a beautiful horse.

    because 后面半句一口气读完。

  • Merrylegs warned me about Ginger's behavior

    warned、behavior 都读对了。

  • I was worried about getting along with Ginger because Merrylegs had warned me about her bad temper.

    长句一口气读下来，没停没错。

  • as we worked together several times

    一处没错。

  • that she was an honest partner

    honest 的 h 不发音，读对了；an honest 连得上。

  • Some mischievous boys annoyed the horses by throwing things

    mischievous 起了个头，第二下就读对了。

  • Feeling curious, I asked him what happened.

    最后一句一口气读完。

  • because I was easier to handle and more comfortable for riding.

    comfortable 一下就出来了，半句没停没错。

  • This made Ginger feel jealous of me.

    一口气读完。

  • The squire always tried to stop cruelty toward animals. Once, we saw a man mistreating a pony.

    cruelty、mistreating 两个长词都是一下读出来的。

  • The weather was cool, and the cart was light.

    一处没错。

  • I did not move because the bridge felt strange and there must be something wrong. Then we were informed that the bridge was broken.

    最后两句 25 个词，没停没错。

  • The squire looked serious, holding a letter in his hand.

    一口气读完，一处没错。

  • It was a good job with good pay and a nice home,

    半句没停没错。

  • Everything was ready except the driving experience, so James had to practice

    experience、practice 两个长词一下读出来。
