🌟 读得好的句子29 句

  • My name is Huckleberry Finn. I had an adventure with my friend Tom Sawyer, and we made a lot of money.

    开头这一句一口气读下来，没停没错。

  • I was homeless, so the Widow Douglas took me in.

    Widow Douglas 是新名字，一次就读出来了。

  • Tom then told us to meet again the following week

    following 读得很清楚。

  • I didn't want to see him because he often beat me.

    最后一句又读顺了。

  • Then he looked around my room

    课本上印的是 look，她读成了 looked —— 读对了，前后都是过去时。

  • Several months passed, and winter came. I was getting used to school and my life with the Widow Douglas.

    开头两句一口气读下来，没停没错。

  • He quickly slapped the book from my hand. “So it's true,” he grumbled.

    slapped、grumbled 两个新词都读对了。

  • I sleep with pigs. I heard you're rich, and that's why I'm here.

    Pap 那几句读得很顺。

  • If they decided to chase the robbers

    先读成 choose，自己发现不对，退回去读成 chase。

  • I intentionally dragged a sack

    五个音节的长词，试了三下，最后读出来了。

  • All the way down to the river. To be safer,

    这一串一口气读下来，没停没错。

  • smashed the cabin door into pieces

    这一句读得很顺。

  • We hid the canoe under the willow branches

    先读成了 widow's，自己发现不对，退回去读成 willow。

  • I left the dead snake curled up on Jim's bed

    先读成 felt，停下来又改成 left —— 两个词字母一样，她自己看出来了。

  • It was a good hiding place and we could keep things dry when it rained. Now we had our own safe shelter.

    两句一口气读下来，没停没错。

  • As I crept closer, I saw a man lying on the ground with a blanket over him.

    这一句读得很顺。

  • I pretended to inquire

    inquire 是个新词，一次读对了。

  • I made up a story about why I was here.

    一句话一口气读下来，没停没错。

  • On the fifth day, he began to feel better.

    这一句读得很顺。

  • Finally, they decided to leave him there

    这半句读得很顺。

  • who was recently murdered

    recently 试了三下，最后读对了。

  • My family tried to cross the river late last night, but we accidentally lost our oars.

    一整句没停没错，accidentally 一次读对。

  • had to float helplessly

    停了快 8 秒，试了几下，最后读对了。

  • When I woke up, the fog had lifted. I saw the raft, and there was Jim, asleep.

    两句连着读下来，很顺。

  • that he had only dreamed everything that happened in the fog.

    最后这句一口气读完。

  • Jim was eager to go to Cairo and be a free man.

    开头这句一口气读下来。

  • The old lady treated me with delicious food and gave me dry clothes.

    treated、gave 都读对了。

  • Unfortunately, the canoe was gone.

    停了 4 秒没放弃，读出来了。

  • I found a note in the Bible, but I kept it a secret.

    最后这句很顺。
