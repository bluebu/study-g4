🌟 读得好的句子31 句

  • One friend said the newspaper described the thief as a gentleman.

    这一句一口气读下来。

  • it was now possible to travel around the world in just eighty days.

    transportation 卡完，后半句接得很顺。

  • However, they were also concerned about Mr. Fogg, as he was most likely to lose the money.

    这句很长，一处没错。

  • as a decent gentleman, he kept his promises.

    最后这句读得稳。

  • Mr. Fogg handed it to Passepartout and asked him to take good care of it because there was twenty-thousand pounds in it.

    这句很长，一处没错。

  • Some people supported Mr. Fogg and believed his journey would be a success.

    一口气读下来。

  • only The Daily Telegraph supported his adventure.

    supported 又读对了一次。

  • Detective Fix was one of them

    换段之后接得稳。

  • He also disclosed his doubt that it was more than a wager.

    这句的 wager 读对了。

  • He was sure that Mr. Fogg was the man the police were looking for.

    一口气读下来，一处没错。

  • Also, catching such a smart criminal would earn a big reward.

    长句，读得稳。

  • Now his name filled the newspapers as a suspect of the bank robbery

    robbery 这次读全了 —— 前两天都读成 robber。

  • On the way to the station, he noticed a magnificent Indian temple

    magnificent 一次读对了。

  • He followed them far from the center of the city.

    一口气读下来，一处没错。

  • As soon as the ship stopped, Fix quickly went to the local police station

    整句读得稳，开头就进了状态。

  • He didn't understand why they took his shoes and was very confused.

    第 73 页三行，一处没错。

  • Sir Francis told them that it was a suttee, a human sacrifice.

    suttee 是个生词，一次读对了。

  • It was midnight, but unfortunately, the guards were awake

    unfortunately 五个音节，一口气读下来。

  • They felt happy and proud of the successful rescue.

    一处没错。

  • They would have enough time to catch the steamer for Hong Kong on October 25 at noon.

    长句，读得稳。

  • She knew it was dangerous for her to stay in India, so she decided to seek refuge with her relative in Hong Kong, China.

    长句一口气读下来，refuge 是生词，读对了。

  • At half past eight, the policeman reappeared and led them to a courtroom.

    reappeared 三个音节，一次读对。

  • Detective Fix managed to board the same boat.

    一处没错。

  • And he realized that Fix must be an agent from the Reform Club

    单词表里的 agent 放进句子里也读对了。

  • He came back with news that her relative had retired and lived in Holland now.

    长句一口气读下来，Holland 读对了。

  • When he learned that Mr. Fogg was determined to continue his journey, he was annoyed.

    determined、journey 都读对了，这句没停没错。

  • He told Mr. Fogg that he was also a passenger on the Rangoon

    passenger 三个音节，一次读对。

  • Passepartout did not believe what Fix said and refused to help him.

    一处没错，refused 的 -ed 也读出来了。

  • It was a miracle that the Tankadere did not sink and survived the challenge of the wild sea.

    长句读下来，miracle、survived 都读对了。

  • At seven, there were still three miles away from Shanghai.

    这句没停没错。

  • It was the American steamer leaving the harbor.

    最后一句一口气读完，American steamer 读得清楚。
